ABOUT R&J PRODUCTIONS

R&J Productions — W Entertainment dba R&J Productions — is dedicated to bringing quality, family-friendly entertainment to our community through free live concerts.

R&J Productions has hosted four free outdoor Summer Concert Series events and plans to host four additional free public concerts during upcoming spring and fall seasons.

Through donations and sponsorships, R&J Productions can continue providing accessible entertainment opportunities that support local talent and vendors and create memorable experiences.
